⚙️ Board processes

Work is a relay — BA hands to Dev, Dev hands to QC — so the same item passes through every process. That is why a process is not defined by a label stuck on an item, but by where the item is right now: each process lists the stages it reads, folding one or more of the autopilot's pipeline columns ({% for col in columns %}{{ col }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}), and ticks your turn on the stages where the ball is in its court. An item moving from In progress to Ready for review leaves Dev's turn and enters QC's — nobody re-tags anything. Folding hides nothing: inside a lane cards stay grouped under the column they are really in, and lanes that are not your turn still show as upstream / downstream context.
▶ Run is the hand-off gate: pick the SDLC profile this process should start, and its cards get a Run button in the lanes that are its turn. The relay stops between roles on purpose, then Dev reads what BA produced and then presses Run — which also removes the parking tags of the lane the card was in. Blank = no button.
The simplest brake needs no ADO change at all: the poller already ignores anything tagged {{ done_tag }}, {{ review_tag_name }} or {{ hold_tag }}, so a finished stage stops itself — park a lane on that tag and Run is exactly "take the tag off and go".
Tags are the exception, not the rule: set them only when one process owns a whole stream end to end (a QC-only backlog, say). Blank = this process sees every item on the board. Saved to config.yaml → board_lenses and applied live; the boards themselves are at Board.
