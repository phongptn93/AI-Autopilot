🔗 Roles

A work item moves role to role, and on this machine every role runs in one process — so which role is due cannot come from the machine, nor from the work-item type (a Bug is a Bug at every step). It comes from where the item stands. Each role below claims the ADO state it waits in, and names the state it leaves the item in when it finishes — one role's way out is the next role's way in. Saved to sdlc_roles, applied live. Boards are at Board.
